DocSigner Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

Summary

DocSigner lets you sign PDF documents, Word documents and pictures on your phone. We do not collect, store or share any personal data. Your documents, scans, photos and signature stay on your device.

What the app processes

  • Documents and pictures you choose (PDF, Word .docx, images): opened and signed on your device only.
  • Your signature: drawn by you and saved only inside the app on your device.
  • Scanned pages: captured with Google's on-device document scanner (provided by Google Play services) or your camera, and converted to a PDF on your device.
  • Signed files: saved in the app's private storage on your device, and anywhere else only when you choose Save to… or Share.

What we collect

Nothing. DocSigner has no account system, no analytics, no advertising and no servers that receive your data. Files leave your phone only when you save them to another location or share them with an app you choose; those apps and services are governed by their own privacy policies.

Permissions

  • Camera: used only when you scan a document or take a photo to sign.
  • Photos: pictures are chosen with Android's photo picker, so DocSigner sees only the picture you select and has no access to your gallery.
  • Files: documents are opened and saved through Android's system file picker, only for the files you select.

Purchases

DocSigner Pro is sold through Google Play. Payments are processed by Google under the Google Privacy Policy. We never receive your payment details; the app only checks with Google Play whether Pro is active.

Children

DocSigner is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

Deleting your data

You can delete signed files in the app at any time. Uninstalling DocSigner removes your signature and all files stored inside the app. Copies you saved elsewhere or shared remain where you put them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
